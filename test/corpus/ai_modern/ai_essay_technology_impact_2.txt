Technological change has accelerated at a remarkable pace, reshaping nearly every aspect of modern life. This essay considers both the opportunities and the risks that accompany this transformation. On the one hand, technology has improved healthcare outcomes, expanded educational access, and connected communities across the globe. On the other hand, it has introduced new forms of inequality and raised difficult ethical questions. To begin with, the concentration of technological capability in a small number of organisations raises concerns about influence and accountability. Additionally, the displacement of workers through automation requires careful social planning. Furthermore, the sheer volume of available information can make it difficult to distinguish reliable sources from unreliable ones. In conclusion, societies must develop appropriate frameworks to ensure that technological progress serves the broader public interest.
